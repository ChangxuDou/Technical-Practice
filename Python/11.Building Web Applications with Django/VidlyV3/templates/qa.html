The project

One idea. A complete journey.

Why does Vidly exist?

Vidly is a personal practice project by Ryandou, a product manager. After self-studying Python, backend development and database modelling, Ryandou connected those skills into a first working platform.

Once the initial flow worked, Ryandou used AI to help improve the interface, extend the business workflows and check how the pieces fit together. The aim is to understand a product all the way from a business requirement and an ER diagram to a usable experience.

What business is this platform designed for?

A physical movie-disc rental store. Customers browse titles and rent available copies; store staff manage customers, stock, returns and settlement.

This is a learning demonstration, not a streaming service or a live rental business. Renting here does not unlock a digital film or arrange an actual delivery.

What connects the experience behind the scenes?

A Django backend connects the catalog, customer accounts, rental records, stock, coupons and wallet. The database design captures how these records relate, while HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript present the experience.

The important part is continuity: renting reduces available stock; returning restores it; receiving new discs adds stock; and a successful simulated payment updates the wallet and rewards together.

02 /

Renting discs

Browse. Rent. Return.

What is the main rental journey?
  1. Browse the movie collection and check the daily rate and available copies.
  2. Add movies to your rental bag, sign in and choose an estimated rental period.
  3. Confirm the rental. Stock is reserved, the daily rate is saved and rental time begins immediately.
  4. In the store scenario, collect the discs and later bring them back to the counter. Collection itself is not tracked as a separate step in this demo.
  5. A cashier inspects the discs, records returns or losses and settles the actual fee. Review the order and receipts in My rentals.
How much does a rental cost?

Normal returns use the daily rate saved at checkout multiplied by the actual rental duration and the number of copies returned. Time is counted in 24-hour periods, rounded up, with a one-day minimum.

For example, one disc at €2 per day returned after 30 hours costs €4 before any coupon. The estimated rental period is a guide; final charges are calculated when staff process the return.

Can I return discs myself or in separate visits?

Only a cashier or manager can record a return after inspecting the physical discs. Customer accounts cannot submit their own returns.

You can return part of an order and bring the rest back later. Each visit has its own settlement, and the remaining copies stay on loan.

What happens if a disc is lost?

Staff mark that copy as lost. The charge is five times its saved daily rate, with no additional rental charge for that lost copy. The store reduces total stock, and the copy does not return to available stock.

Coupons cannot reduce lost-disc charges.

03 /

Beyond rentals

Help shape the next movie night.

What if a movie is missing from the catalog?

Submit its title and release year in your Wishlist, with an optional note. Matching titles and years are grouped together, and each customer counts once.

The manager sees aggregated demand and can plan a purchase or share an update. Once staff receive the discs, the wishlist status changes and you can open the movie to check live availability. A request is an expression of interest, not a reservation or a promise to purchase.

How do wallet top-ups and reward points work?

Enter an amount in your Wallet, then choose success, failure or cancellation in the payment simulator. Only success credits your balance and points. No bank card or real payment provider is involved.

The manager sets the money-to-points rate. The rate is saved when a top-up order is created and remains valid for 30 minutes. Points are proportional to the amount, rounded down to whole points.

For example, at 100 points per €10, a €25 top-up earns 250 points. Points are currently for display only; wallet balance can be used by staff at rental settlement.

How do I claim and use a coupon?

Enter a valid coupon code in My rentals. The demo includes WELCOME10 for 10% off rental fees. Each customer can claim a coupon once and use it for one settlement before it expires.

Ask the cashier to apply it when returning discs. Coupons reduce rental fees only, not lost-disc charges. With a partial return, a coupon is used for that settlement rather than saved for the whole order.

Can a wallet balance be refunded?

Staff can record a simulated balance refund, up to the available balance. Reward points from customer top-ups are reversed proportionally using the original awards.

Staff-entered top-ups do not earn reward points, and rental spending does not award additional points. These actions record demo transactions; they do not move real money.

04 /

Behind the counter

The right tools for each role.

What can customers, cashiers and managers do?
  • Customers: browse, rent, check orders, submit wishes, simulate top-ups and claim coupons.
  • Cashiers: register and manage customers, check out rentals by barcode, process returns and losses, settle fees, record wallet adjustments and receive approved purchases.
  • Managers: use cashier tools, manage movies and stock, review wishlist demand, create purchase orders, configure coupons and reward policies, and review performance reports.
  • Superusers: access administration and manage accounts and permissions, as well as manager workflows.
How do customer wishes become new stock?

The manager reviews demand and creates a purchase order for a new movie or an existing title that needs restocking. Creating the order does not increase inventory.

When discs arrive, a manager or cashier checks the delivery and records the quantity received. Both total and available stock increase, and linked wishes are updated. Deliveries can be received in batches; duplicate submissions do not add the same stock twice.

What can managers configure and measure?

Managers can set movie availability and daily rates, manage coupon codes, discounts and expiry dates, and configure the top-up rewards rate or pause new top-ups. Reward policy changes keep an audit record and apply only to new top-up orders.

Reports show daily, monthly and yearly settlement revenue, total rental orders, popular movies and top customers. Revenue includes lost-disc charges and deducts coupon discounts; top-ups are excluded. The reporting time zone is Europe/Berlin.

Which parts are simulated?

Payments, cash settlements, wallet refunds and supplier purchases are local demonstrations. No real bank, payment provider or supplier is contacted. The demo does not stream movies, ship discs or redeem points for rent.

The records and stock movements inside the platform do work together, so you can explore the complete business journey with demo data.
